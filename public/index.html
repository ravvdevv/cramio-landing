GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

The useful details, without the long sales pitch.

Can I use Cramio on the web?

Yes, and if you're not on Android, it's the way in. Open the web app in your browser on iPhone, iPad, or desktop and sign in to the same study space, same account.

Does Cramio work offline?

Some content stored on your device may be available offline. AI chat, sign-in, and cloud sync need an internet connection.

Is there an Android app?

Yes. Cramio is Android-first: the native Android app, plus a web app that opens in any browser right now.

What can I study with Cramio?

Bring your notes and PDFs, ask questions about topics, and review with quizzes and flashcards. You choose what to study and when.
