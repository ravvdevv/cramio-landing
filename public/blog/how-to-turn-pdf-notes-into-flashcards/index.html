THE PROBLEM WITH PDFs

Reading isn't the same as remembering

You can read a 30-page textbook chapter three times and still blank out during the exam. Reading feels like learning, but it's passive. What actually sticks is active recall — retrieving information from memory, not re-reading it.

The problem: making flashcards from a PDF takes forever. You read, you highlight, you type out questions and answers. By the time you're done, you're too tired to study.

WHY THIS WORKS

From passive to active

Re-reading a PDF is passive. Reviewing flashcards is active recall — your brain has to retrieve the answer, which strengthens the memory trace. AI conversion removes the busy work so you spend time studying, not formatting.
